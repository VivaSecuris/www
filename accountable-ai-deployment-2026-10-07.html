VivaSecuris Develops Security Controls for Accountable AI Deployment

October 7, 2026

VivaSecuris is developing security infrastructure designed to limit access to sensitive resources and make access decisions auditable, with healthcare as its initial application.

As AI systems become part of operational workflows, organizations need to understand which identities can access which resources and how those permissions are enforced. VivaSecuris’s development work focuses on identity verification, sensitive-workload isolation and auditable access through its SecurisNexus platform.

VivaSecuris also offers medical-device security assessment services. The company welcomes conversations with clinical IT and security teams, device manufacturers and researchers interested in discussing security needs and potential design partnerships.

To request a conversation, contact founder Joshua Hill at josh@vivasecuris.com.

About VivaSecuris

VivaSecuris develops security infrastructure for sensitive environments, starting with healthcare, and offers medical-device security assessment services. VivaSecuris is the brand of VIVASECURIS SYNDICATE, a Delaware corporation.
