Company background

Security for sensitive environments.

VivaSecuris develops security infrastructure for sensitive environments, starting with healthcare, and offers medical-device security assessment services.

Our development work focuses on identity verification, sensitive-workload isolation and auditable access.

VivaSecuris is the brand of VIVASECURIS SYNDICATE, a Delaware corporation. Joshua Hill is the company’s founder.
