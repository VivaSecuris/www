The work behind these posts

We write about healthcare security because it is the work we do. Six services are available today, each scoped to fit around care delivery rather than interrupt it.

  • See what’s on your network — every device and every remote login, including vendors and IT support, with a record auditors and insurers can use. We observe; we never block care.
  • Security testing & assessment — we test your systems the way an attacker would and hand you a report you can use with insurers and regulators.
  • Medical device security — firmware, software, and network risk review for hospitals and manufacturers. Submitting a cyber device to FDA? Start with our 524B evidence checklist.
  • AI systems before you use them in care — safety and security review of AI and chatbots before they reach patients.
  • Security training — role-based sessions for clinicians, biomed, and IT, tied to issues we actually see — risk reduction, not compliance theater.
  • Who can open a chart — access as an explicit decision: who, which device, how long, with a record you can explain later.
