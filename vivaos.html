Where we're headed: VivaOS

VivaSecuris builds operating-system-level zero-trust infrastructure for clinical networks — so hospitals can enforce identity, isolate patient data, and audit every access event without disrupting the care workflows those protections are supposed to defend. VivaOS is the platform that work is heading toward.

Talk to us about the platform What we offer today

VivaOS is in development. This page describes design direction — what we are building toward — not deployed capability. What we offer today is listed under Solutions.

Problems we are designing for

Designed around how healthcare actually runs—and the people it protects

🔒

Patient data mixed up or leaked?

When one patient's information shows up in another's chart, trust and safety break. VivaOS is designed so each patient's records are encrypted per record and released only on an explicit policy decision. That boundary is a design requirement we intend to prove workflow by workflow, not a guarantee that mix-ups cannot happen.

📋

Billing and scheduling a mess?

Appointments, insurance, and payments scattered across systems mean errors, delays, and lost revenue. Our design goal is for scheduling, verification, billing, and payments to run inside the same identity and audit model as clinical data. This is roadmap, not a shipped module.

📦

Supplies and inventory guesswork?

Running out of what you need—or drowning in what you don't—slows care and wastes money. We are designing supply, medication, and asset tracking to share that same foundation, so operations inherit the same protections. Roadmap, not yet built.

🖥️

Clinicians jumping between five screens?

When the work is split across too many tools, care slows down and mistakes creep in. VivaOS is designed around a small number of clinical surfaces that follow the way clinicians already work. Less screen-switching is a requirement we measure against, not a result we have proven in a live ward.

🛡️

Security an afterthought?

Bolting security onto old systems leaves gaps. VivaOS is designed with identity, encryption, access control, and tamper-evident audit records as the foundation rather than an add-on, so the evidence auditors and insurers ask for is produced by normal work. Which events are captured, and how completely, is something we document per workflow rather than assert.

What the platform is designed to include

Clinical, financial, and operations surfaces on one security foundation — in development

🏥

Clinical workflows

Patient dashboard, medication administration, and vitals entry are the first clinical surfaces we are building, designed around workflows clinicians already know. Not yet in clinical use.

💰

Revenue cycle & billing

Scheduling, insurance verification, billing, and patient payments are on the roadmap, designed to inherit the same identity, permission, and audit model as clinical data. Not yet built.

📦

Operations & inventory

Tracking for supplies, medications, and equipment is planned on the same foundation, so what you have and where it is can be answered with the same evidence as any other access. Not yet built.

🔐

Privacy & security built in

Designed so every session, device, and AI agent presents a cryptographic identity and receives an explicit, time-bounded policy grant before touching patient data; per-record encryption released only on a policy decision; hash-chained, tamper-evident audit records. Which of these is enforced today, and where, is documented per workflow — ask us.

Why we're different

Security is the foundation we are building on, not a layer added later

Most healthcare systems were built for features first and security later, and it shows: breaches, mix-ups, and audit headaches. We started from the position that privacy and security are a right, not a privilege. Every part of VivaOS — clinical, billing, inventory — is being designed so data stays protected, care stays available, and the evidence exists to show both. We would rather demonstrate one bounded workflow with its evidence than describe a finished system, and we hold ourselves to the scrutiny we ask you to apply to any vendor.

So security failures don't become patient harm

Live a safe life. VivaOS is the platform we are building toward that goal: care, operations, and security on one foundation, designed so clinicians can focus on patients and organizations can account for every access. Today we get there one monitored network, one assessment, and one proven workflow at a time.
