The 524B Premarket Cybersecurity Evidence Checklist

The technical artifacts FDA reviewers actually want — and the gaps that get submissions Refuse-to-Accept'd.

By Joshua Hill · VivaSecuris

Since October 2023, FDA can Refuse to Accept any premarket submission for a "cyber device" that's missing cybersecurity content — and under the current final guidance, cyber deficiencies are now one of the top reasons submissions stall across 510(k), PMA, De Novo, HDE, and PDP. Most teams have a cybersecurity plan. Far fewer have the evidence that makes the plan credible to a reviewer. This checklist is organized around the artifacts you must be able to hand over — not just the paperwork. If you can't check a box with something you could give a reviewer today, that's a likely RTA finding.
