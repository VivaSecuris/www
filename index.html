Where AI security fits

AI used in care meets the same bar as a device used in care.

AI is part of treatment now, so it has to show what it does before it touches a patient — the bar a medical device already meets. You can only check an AI you can open and measure. That is why our research runs on open weights, on your own machine, and measures what can be removed from the file, not a refusal score against someone else’s API. A model that fails in care is a security failure, and security failures must not become patient harm.
